Moving services built around your move

Whatever you're moving and however far, one trained Haul crew handles it end to end. Explore each service, estimate your cost, and book — all on this page.

Local Moving

Local moves across the metro

For any move under 50 miles, we send a right-sized crew and truck and bill by the hour — with a written not-to-exceed cap so your price is protected. Same-day and next-day slots are available, and most one to three-bedroom homes are done in a single day.

  • Uniformed, background-checked crew
  • Truck, blankets, straps & dollies
  • Furniture disassembly & reassembly
  • Free wardrobe boxes on move day
  • Not-to-exceed hourly cap
  • On-time guarantee

Long-Distance Moving

Long-distance, done right

Cross-state or cross-country, you get a dedicated truck and the same crew door to door — no swapping trailers or mixing your things with other shipments. Every long-distance move is a flat, guaranteed price with a real delivery window, plus text updates the whole way.

  • Dedicated truck & one crew
  • Guaranteed delivery window
  • Flat, locked-in pricing
  • Full packing available
  • Real-time move updates
  • Full-value protection option

Packing & Unpacking

Packing that saves your weekend

Our packers box up your whole home in a fraction of the time, using quality materials and color-coded labels so every carton lands in the right room. Choose full-service packing and unpacking, partial packing for just the fragile stuff, or simply buy supplies and do it yourself.

  • Full or partial packing
  • Quality boxes, paper & tape
  • Color-coded room labeling
  • Dish & wardrobe barrels
  • Unpacking at destination
  • Debris & box removal

Storage

Secure storage between moves

When your dates don't line up, we hold your belongings in clean, climate-controlled, 24/7-monitored vaults billed by the month. Everything is inventoried on the way in and re-delivered to your new home whenever you're ready — so you never handle it twice.

  • Climate-controlled vaults
  • 24/7 monitored & secured
  • Billed simply by the month
  • Full inventory on intake
  • Re-delivery when you're ready
  • Short- or long-term

Office moves without the downtime

We relocate offices, retail, and warehouses after hours and on weekends so your team is back up and running fast. From labeling every workstation to disconnecting and reconnecting IT, a dedicated project manager plans the whole move around your business.

  • After-hours & weekend crews
  • Dedicated project manager
  • IT disconnect & reconnect
  • Workstation labeling & plans
  • Certificates of insurance
  • Furniture install & teardown
